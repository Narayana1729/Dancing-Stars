# Original Similipal Boundary Datasets

This directory contains pristine, unmodified source datasets obtained from official and international repositories for Similipal Tiger Reserve, Odisha.

## Files

1. `Notification_Similipal.pdf`
   - **Source:** National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA), Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Govt. of India (`https://ntca.gov.in/tiger-reserves/`).
   - **Content:** Official statutory gazette notification for Similipal Tiger Reserve under Section 38V of the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972. Includes Schedule A (Statutory areas: Core/CTH 1,194.75 km², Buffer 1,555.25 km², Total 2,750.00 km²) and Schedule B (Metes-and-bounds outer boundary survey description).
   - **Retrieved:** 2026-11-14

2. `wdpa_similipal_raw_4326.geojson`
   - **Source:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) via UNEP-WCMC World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA), Layer 1 (`WDPA_poly_latest`).
   - **CRS:** EPSG:4326 (WGS 84 geographic latitude/longitude).
   - **Features:** 11 gazetted polygon features covering the Core Similipal Reserve Forest (Site ID: 556167801) and 7 peripheral wildlife ranges (Barehipani, Chahala, Gurguria, Kendumundi, Nawana North, Talabandha, Thakurmunda).
   - **Retrieved:** 2026-11-14
